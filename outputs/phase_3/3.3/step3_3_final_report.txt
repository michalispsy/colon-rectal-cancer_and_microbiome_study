Method
------------------------------------------------------------------------
For each rCLR species and each study, the script computes Hedges' g
for Group 1 minus Group 0. Hedges' g is a standardized mean difference
with a small-sample correction. Per-study SMDs are then combined across
studies using an intercept-only random-effects model with REML tau²
estimation. The report includes pooled effect size, standard error, 95% CI,
p-value, Benjamini-Hochberg q-value, Cochran's Q, Q p-value, tau², and I².

Minimum per-study group size: 3 per group
A species/comparison requires at least two valid studies to be meta-analyzed.
Positive effect means higher rCLR abundance in Group 1.

Interpretation guide
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Hedges' g > 0 means higher rCLR abundance in the first group of the comparison.
  Example: CRC_vs_Control with g > 0 means higher in CRC.
- Hedges' g < 0 means higher in the second group.
- I² describes the fraction of between-study heterogeneity not explained by sampling error.
  High I² means the species effect is cohort-dependent and should be interpreted cautiously.
- Sex-stratified analyses are exploratory if few studies contain enough male/female samples
  in both comparison groups.
