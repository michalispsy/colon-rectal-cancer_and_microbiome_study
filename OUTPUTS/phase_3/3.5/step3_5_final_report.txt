METHODS
For each rCLR species, the script computed mean and median abundance in Control, Adenoma, and CRC.
It then tested the ordered trend Control < Adenoma < CRC using a Jonckheere-Terpstra test.
The reverse ordered trend Control > Adenoma > CRC was also tested to detect decreasing sequence markers.
Pairwise Mann-Whitney tests and Cliff's delta were computed for CRC vs Control, Adenoma vs Control, and CRC vs Adenoma.
All p-values were corrected with Benjamini-Hochberg FDR correction.

CATEGORY DEFINITIONS
EARLY: significant in Adenoma vs Control and CRC vs Control.
LATE: significant in CRC vs Control but not Adenoma vs Control.
PROGRESSION: significant in CRC vs Adenoma.
ORDERED_INCREASING: significant Jonckheere-Terpstra trend Control < Adenoma < CRC and monotonic group means.
ORDERED_DECREASING: significant Jonckheere-Terpstra trend Control > Adenoma > CRC and monotonic group means.

INTERPRETATION GUIDANCE
If many ORDERED_INCREASING or ORDERED_DECREASING markers are found, this supports a gradual microbiome shift across Control -> Adenoma -> CRC.
If LATE markers dominate, the microbiome signal appears stronger at the carcinoma stage than at the adenoma stage.
If EARLY markers dominate, adenoma already carries CRC-relevant microbial changes.
If PROGRESSION markers are rare, CRC and Adenoma may be hard to separate at species-level rCLR abundance.
Because Adenoma samples usually occur in fewer cohorts, interpret sequence findings as exploratory unless replicated by study-blocked or LODO analyses.
